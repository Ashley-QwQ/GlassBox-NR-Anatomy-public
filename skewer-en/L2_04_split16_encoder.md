[split16](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g013) refers to the 16-head, [512](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g007)-channel [window](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g015)-block [family](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g080). [ffwd](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g037) / ffwd_proj / qkv / [proj](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g038) are the [role](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g084) interpretations for the feed-forward front half / back half, [query](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g040)-key-value with fused [attention](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g039), and output projection; for [full](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g018), pooled, N_out, [E4](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g055)/[F16](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g054), [K32](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g065), and [seed](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g042), see the [notation table](INDEX.md#shape-and-workspace-notation). X₅=60×36 and X₆=32×20 are the 1080 example on this page, in width × height; primary roles are E4, and auxiliary role types are read per [descriptor](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g088).

## Shared Implementation Contract and Family-Specific Computation

[CPU Reconstruction / CENSUS] Each family obtains its roles from the current [params](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g083) and descriptors, and completes [matrices](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g051), [reductions](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g050), and [publications](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g091) according to the current [frozen](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g103) graph; see the lower level for concrete fetch/writeback indices. Optional classes, exchange sources, and branch tables are bound per instance. Writeback ownership is unique, exits are sliced into independent pointers according to each role's length, and completion flags are then published. This is the common execution method and does not replace the [operator](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g035) differences in the table below.

- **How ffwd / ffwd_inpview is computed:** Receives a 512-channel input, first performs a 512→512 pre-projection and [quantizes](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g061), then executes G and contraction across eight groups of 64→256→64 according to the named graph, publishing 512-channel E4; the first block fetches via the [TIN](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g011) pool input view, and subsequent blocks use the [chained](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g022) view. The intra-kernel total gate width is 2048, which does not equal the [physical](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g085) N_out; for the grouping basis and matrix accounting, see the [FFN table](L3_ffn.md#channels).
- **How ffwd_proj / inpview variant is computed:** Consumes the 512-channel E4 from the current block's ffwd and performs a 512→512 post-projection; the same block's entry is [decoded](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g061) and multiplied by a half [scale](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g031) to serve as the half seed of the first K32, publishing E4 after 16 steps. It cannot be replaced by an identically shaped [residual](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g017) from an adjacent block; see [residual interfaces](L3_ffn.md#residual) for details.
- **How qkv is computed:** Consumes the post-feed-forward [features](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g008) and completes Q/K/V and fused attention within the current shifted window; indices must be rebound whenever the window shift changes, and only repeated shifts can reuse an address map. wload_off, sread_shared_off, sread_src, xsite_src, xsite_wsrc, gstore_off, shfl_idx, and psel have [eight-field closed forms validated on held-out maps](L3_split16_window.md#qkv-closed-form-r04n); thread_class remains table-driven because the field is absent in 1080 and the frozen hypothesis failed on held-out validation. The production executor still reads the original tables, and address equivalence does not expand into a proof of the numeric DAG, full chain, or dynamic provenance. It is not a standalone 512→1536 linear transformation, and internal role lengths cannot be treated across the board as three copies of Q/K/V; for the documented scores, reduction, [RCP](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g072), and [AV](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g077), see the [attention breakdown](L3_attention_softmax.md#window-attention).
- **How proj / proj_pool is computed:** Consumes the current block's qkv exit and the post-feed-forward [bypass](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g016), forming the next block's trunk according to the projection/merge graph; L56 forms the spatial full exit and the pooled re-gridded exit separately via two sets of writeback indices, and one cannot compute only a single skip and substitute unverified library pooling for the other. The name pooled does not prove a standard average-pooling formula.
- **How final_head is computed:** Consumes L56 pooled, executes an independent graph according to the current 2D grid, [weights](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g079), and gather indices, and publishes a 1024-channel role; its output pointer is the exact L58 input pointer, with no subsequent [ViT](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g014) residual input and no classification-head semantics.
- **Numeric rules:** Matrices publish F16 at each K32; nodes for reductions, the [gate function](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g045), half/E4 quantization, fusion, and residual seeds preserve the original order of the frozen graph; out-of-table RSQ/RCP inputs are rejected, and QMMA [overflow](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g071) guards are not removed. Confirmed seeds are given in the table below and in L3; an unknown physical layout does not relax arithmetic ordering.
- **Why:** ffwd adds hidden roles for per-position processing, and ffwd_proj brings them back to the trunk while preserving the entry; qkv exchanges positional content within windows, and proj forms the next block's features; proj_pool retains the pre-[bottleneck](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g010) spatial bypass while reducing [tokens](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g041), and final_head adapts to the bottleneck width. These are the operator roles of projection, grouping, residuals, and re-gridding; the training intent behind choosing these widths and fusion boundaries remains unknown.


### Address Closed Forms and Held-Out Boundaries for the Remaining Families

<a id="audit-family-ids"></a>
Below, F16–F23 are audit family IDs; when F16 is used as a family ID, it does not denote the F16/half floating-point format of the same name. In F18.36, F18 denotes the family and 36 denotes the audit item within that family.

The [per-field executable formulas and evidence tables](L3_split16_window.md#rest-closed-form-r04o) for F16 (inpview ffwd), F17 (inpview ffwd_proj), F19 (proj), F20 (ffwd), F21 (ffwd_proj), F22 (proj_pool), and F23 (final_head) share a 4×4 physical block P_C; out-of-bounds sources −1 are zero-padded, out-of-bounds writebacks −1 are skipped, and −3 in xsite selects a resource. The ordinary 64-event resource-plane offset is `16384*(e//4)+2048*h+512*(e%4)+16*lane`; F23 changes the basket stride to 32768, and the latter 64 events of F16/F20 are further split into expansion/contraction. F22 additionally has pooled second-exit indices and exchange/selection formulas, and F23 writes back in 1024-channel output blocks.

Before reading the English labels below, you may first check the [address-formula and comparison-label legend](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#address-status).

Read each field separately as EXACT, historical r02 FAIL, or VACUOUS: the historical variants of sread_src in F16/F17 and wload/xsite in F19 have been classified by r04 as NON_PRODUCTION_VARIANT, with their corresponding accepted maps EXACT and the r02 failures left unrewritten; no receipt proves that C++ actually reads those 12×12 maps. Empty xsite arrays in F16/F20/F23 do not constitute non-empty address evidence, and thread_class remains TABLE. The 68×120 4K tables for F16/F17/F19–F22 are EXACT, and 4K for F18 comes from the independent r01 QKV formula; F23 additionally has a 36×60 bound table, without an independently bound 4K frame identity. See [per-family counts](L3_split16_window.md#r04-four-k-r04p) for details; address equality does not prove the numeric DAG, full chain, or dynamic provenance.
