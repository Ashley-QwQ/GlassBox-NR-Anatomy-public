This page collects existing records supporting the statement that "fixed inputs, controls, [resources](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g079), and pre-states produce reproducible bytes", with each conclusion bounded by its observation scope. Unified M, the release sub-[seal](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g103) [M2](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g107), and subsequent child candidates are accounted for separately; measured results on M cannot be automatically transferred as new measurements on M2, nor can census substitute for numeric runs. ## Driver and Rental-Machine Identity

**[measured, historical summary; not bound to M / M2 / child candidate numeric gates]** Earlier records report that identically labeled native outputs under 616.92 and 591.86 are byte-for-byte identical, [README_ZH.md:20](../../../README_ZH.md#L20); the [architecture](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g078) document restricts this to a "stale, single-item" record along the driver dimension, [ARCHITECTURE_V3_ZH.md:1391](../../../ARCHITECTURE_V3_ZH.md#L1391). Across the sources specified for this round and their explicit citations, **the original capture pairing table, full input/parameter identity gate, and M/M2 retests corresponding to those two driver versions are not recorded**. Therefore only this historical observation is retained, and one cannot declare from it that all drivers, all intermediate [states](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g002), or arbitrary inputs are equal.

**[census, rental rule; native reference tier, non-M / M2 / child candidate numeric gate]** RTX 5090 D V2 indeed appears in the rental records, with the on-site name, UUID, driver 580.97, and sm_120 all registered; the user's rental-identity rule is that name, UUID, and driver are only recorded and not blocked by their strings, while actual [admission](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g101) is determined by the result bytes of the frozen positive control. [LATEST_ZH.md:130](../../../LATEST_ZH.md#L130).

**[measured, native reference tier; non-M / M2 / child candidate new acceptance]** Subsequent archive review records that 5090 D V2 / 580.97 completed seven gates across seven runs, all matching the 5090 / 591.86 baseline byte for byte at the [FINAL](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g105) tier; the report simultaneously retains the limitations that "only one card was tested, and the provenance of the frozen expected outputs was not traced back to the original capture in that round". [experiments/kb-r04-register-r01-20261011-claude/RESULT_ZH.md:7–14, 65](../../../experiments/kb-r04-register-r01-20261011-claude/RESULT_ZH.md#L7). Another named redemption record also explicitly restricts the positive control and prediction redemption on that device to frame 0 and the final-image tier, [experiments/b1-preregistered-bright-scene-redeem-20261018/RESULT_ZH.md:26–30](../../../experiments/b1-preregistered-bright-scene-redeem-20261018/RESULT_ZH.md#L26). These records support the positive control for that device configuration and cannot be generalized to all SKUs or future drivers.

<a id="workspace-fill"></a>
## What the 00 and A5 Fills Actually Test

**[measured on M]** The 4K own-state CPU continuous chain ran f0→f1→f2 from [reset](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g032) for each fill; the three final images under both fills each match the same native reference, at 66,355,200 B per image. That round was bound to M=9d0a4593… without injecting native intermediate states. [experiments/parity-cpp-4k-multiframe-r01-20261022-codex/RESULT_ZH.md:19, 34–40](../../../experiments/parity-cpp-4k-multiframe-r01-20261022-codex/RESULT_ZH.md#L19).

The comparison scopes must be distinguished: the 00 side compares FULL as reported; the A5 side at early launches checks that the cumulative written region matches the native reference while the unwritten [complement](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g093) remains A5. Both fills cumulatively cover the entire 763,062,272 B [workspace](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g082) only starting at f0 L99, while f1/f2 are fully covered starting from [L0](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g081); both f0 L0 FULL states are [NOT_OBSERVED](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g104). [experiments/parity-cpp-4k-multiframe-r01-20261022-codex/RESULT_ZH.md:32](../../../experiments/parity-cpp-4k-multiframe-r01-20261022-codex/RESULT_ZH.md#L32). Earlier 1080 records also make clear that the digests of the cumulative declared write sets under the two fills match at 158/158 points, whereas FULL matches only at the 59 points of L99–L157, [ARCHITECTURE_V3_ZH.md:118–125](../../../ARCHITECTURE_V3_ZH.md#L118); those earlier numbers are not relabeled here as an M2 dual-fill measurement.

**[inferred from measured on M and prior captures]** These controlled comparisons rule out the hypothesis that "on the measured trajectory, replacing 00 with A5 changes the compared outputs", supporting the conclusion that the measured results do not depend on an accidental choice between those two initial values; they do not prove that uninitialized regions are never read, nor do they rule out values being read and then overwritten, masked out, or happening not to affect the result, and still less do they prove independence across arbitrary initial values, inputs, [aliasing](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g092), or schedules. The original project definition explicitly restricts the two fills to a finite falsification test, [ARCHITECTURE_V3_ZH.md:121–125](../../../ARCHITECTURE_V3_ZH.md#L121); for read/write and synchronization proof boundaries, see also the [appendix](APPENDIX_evidence_history.md#project-terms).

## Repeated Runs and Same-Capture Reproduction

**[measured, historical native capture; non-M / M2 / child candidate gate]** The REDG double-run check obtained zero differences across 787 paired checkpoints, including 157 points on frame 0 and 158 points each on frames 1 and 2; however, the original report status is INCONCLUSIVE_INPUT_PARAMS_GATE because the original P1/P2 uploads are missing and some parameter differences exceeded the predeclared exceptions, so it did not establish a native reproducibility conclusion under "identical inputs and identical parameters". [experiments/parity-redg-double-run-1080-r01-20260929-codex/RESULT_ZH.md:7–22](../../../experiments/parity-redg-double-run-1080-r01-20260929-codex/RESULT_ZH.md#L7). A later census lists four comparable frame-0 captures, reordered [negative controls](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g102), and post-hoc relocation corrections side by side, while still retaining the gap that the original uploads reach only the catalog SHA, [experiments/parity-proof-structure-census-r01-20261006-codex/RESULT_ZH.md:15](../../../experiments/parity-proof-structure-census-r01-20261006-codex/RESULT_ZH.md#L15).

**[inferred, native ordering obligation; not a full-domain theorem for M / M2 / child candidate]** Agreement across finite repeats does not prove that all arrival orders, synchronizations, or visibility behaviors are identical; an independent ordering certificate remains a separate obligation. [docs/arch-v2/08.01_redg.md:32](../../arch-v2/08.01_redg.md#L32).

**[measured on M]** In the n=2 own-state CPU reproduction of 4K f0/f1 against the same fixed capture, all 632/632 [launch](APPENDIX_beginner_glossary.md#g080) pairs, 1264/1264 non-volatile receipt projections, and 7584/7584 state SHAs match. The comparison retains all numeric SHAs, routing, and write sets, excluding only volatile metadata such as run IDs and timings according to a pre-frozen schema; f2 is a new measurement not included in the n=2 denominator, and no claim is made that the raw JSON files are identical in full. [experiments/parity-cpp-4k-multiframe-r01-20261022-codex/RESULT_ZH.md:44–46](../../../experiments/parity-cpp-4k-multiframe-r01-20261022-codex/RESULT_ZH.md#L44). This is a CPU reproduction against the same reference and cannot serve as a second native GPU capture.
